Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

1. Introduction

NyumbaManager ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA 2019), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable, and all applicable privacy regulations. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Identity Data: Full name, National ID number, passport number, date of birth, gender, profile photo.
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, physical and postal address.
  • Financial Data: Rent invoices, payment records, M-Pesa transaction references, bank transfer references. We never store your M-Pesa PIN or bank credentials.
  • Property Data: Unit details, lease terms, maintenance requests, inspection reports, inventory records.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, MAC addresses (where technically available via custom device headers), browser type and version, device fingerprint, operating system, screen resolution, timezone, language settings, referrer URLs, pages visited, and session data.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the platform, features used, time stamps of actions.
  • Communications: Messages, maintenance request descriptions, announcements.
  • Document Data: Uploaded lease agreements, identification documents, receipts, photos.

⚠ For super administrators only: full forensic visitor logs including IP addresses, device fingerprints, geolocation data, and browser metadata are stored and accessible to monitor platform security and prevent fraud.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases (DPA 2019, Section 30):

  • Contract performance: To provide rent management, payment processing, and maintenance services under your lease or service agreement.
  • Legal obligation: To comply with Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) requirements and financial record-keeping laws.
  • Legitimate interests: To prevent fraud, ensure platform security, and improve our services.
  • Consent: For optional SMS notifications, email marketing (which you may withdraw at any time).

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Generating and sending rent invoices
  • Processing M-Pesa and bank payments (as an orchestration layer only — we never hold your funds)
  • Sending SMS and email payment receipts, reminders, and maintenance updates via Africa's Talking
  • Managing leases, tenancy agreements, and property documents
  • Facilitating maintenance request tracking and resolution
  • Providing landlords with P&L reports and reconciliation dashboards
  • Platform security monitoring and fraud prevention (forensic logs)
  • KRA-ready financial reporting exports

5. Data Sharing

We share your data with:

  • Safaricom (M-Pesa Daraja API): For processing M-Pesa STK Push payment requests. Your phone number and payment amount are shared to initiate transactions.
  • Africa's Talking: Your phone number and message content for SMS delivery.
  • Equity Bank / KCB / Co-op Bank APIs: For statement reconciliation, where you have explicitly authorised this.
  • Property managers and landlords: Your tenancy and payment details as necessary for the provision of rental services.
  • Law enforcement: Where required by Kenyan law, court order, or for investigation of fraud.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Active account data: For the duration of your account plus 7 years (KRA financial record-keeping requirement)
  • Visitor logs and forensic data: 12 months
  • Payment records: 7 years
  • Lease documents: 10 years
  • SMS logs: 6 months

7. Your Rights (DPA 2019)

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and your applicable regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: At any time, for consent-based processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@nyumbamanager.co.ke

8. Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest; TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Bcrypt password hashing (cost factor 13)
  • HTTP security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, etc.)
  • Rate limiting on all authentication endpoints
  • Account lockout after 5 failed login attempts
  • CSRF protection on all state-changing requests
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) — tenants cannot access landlord data
  • Full audit logging of all sensitive actions
  • Local Kenyan cloud hosting for data sovereignty

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management (authentication), and functional cookies to remember your theme preference. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
