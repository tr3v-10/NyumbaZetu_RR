Service Level Agreement

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

This SLA defines the service standards NyumbaManager commits to for all paid subscription plans.

Uptime

We target 99.5% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance windows (announced at least 24 hours in advance). This equates to no more than 3.65 hours downtime per month.

Response Times

  • Critical (platform down, payment failures): 1 hour response, 4 hours resolution
  • High (major feature unavailable): 4 hours response, 24 hours resolution
  • Medium (minor feature issue): 1 business day response, 3 business days resolution
  • Low (cosmetic / enhancement): 5 business days response

M-Pesa Callbacks

STK Push callbacks from Safaricom are processed within 30 seconds of receipt. Automatic receipt generation occurs within 60 seconds of a confirmed M-Pesa payment.

Support Channels

Email: support@nyumbamanager.co.ke · Phone: +254 700 000 000 (Mon–Fri 8am–6pm EAT) · Emergency: +254 700 000 001 (24/7 for critical issues)

Credits

Failure to meet uptime targets entitles customers to service credits: 10% of monthly fee for each 0.5% below the 99.5% target.

Questions? Contact us at legal@nyumbamanager.co.ke
