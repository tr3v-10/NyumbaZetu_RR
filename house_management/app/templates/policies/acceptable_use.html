Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

By using NyumbaManager, you agree to use it only for lawful, ethical property management activities. This Acceptable Use Policy supplements our Terms of Service.

You Must Not

  • Harass, threaten, or discriminate against tenants, landlords, or staff via the messaging system
  • Upload obscene, defamatory, or illegal content
  • Use fake identities or forged documents
  • Manipulate payment records or receipts
  • Use the platform to evade legal obligations including tax (KRA)
  • Attempt SQL injection, XSS, or any security exploits

Landlord Obligations

Landlords must: obtain tenant consent before uploading their personal data; comply with Kenya's Landlord and Tenant Act; provide receipts for all rent payments; and maintain properties to habitability standards.

Reporting Violations

Report violations to abuse@nyumbamanager.co.ke. We investigate all reports and reserve the right to remove content and suspend accounts.

Questions? Contact us at legal@nyumbamanager.co.ke
