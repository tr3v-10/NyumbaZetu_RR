Data Protection Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

NyumbaManager processes personal data in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 (DPA 2019) and is registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Our Commitments

  • Data minimisation — we only collect what is necessary
  • Purpose limitation — data is used only for stated purposes
  • Storage limitation — data is retained for defined periods only
  • Integrity and confidentiality — robust technical and organisational measures
  • Accountability — we maintain records of all processing activities

Data Transfers

All primary data is stored on servers within Kenya. Where data is transmitted to third-party APIs (Safaricom, Africa's Talking, Equity Bank), adequate safeguards are in place as required by DPA 2019 Section 48.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and the ODPC within 72 hours as required by DPA 2019 Section 43.
