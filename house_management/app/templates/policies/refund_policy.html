Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

This policy covers refunds for NyumbaManager subscription fees. It does not govern rent refunds between landlords and tenants (which are governed by individual lease agreements and Kenya's Landlord and Tenant Act).

Subscription Fees

Monthly subscriptions: Refunds are available within 7 days of the billing date if the service was not used. Annual subscriptions: Pro-rata refunds are available for unused months upon cancellation.

Non-Refundable Items

  • SMS credits consumed via Africa's Talking
  • Setup and onboarding fees
  • Fees for months where the service was actively used

How to Request

Email billing@nyumbamanager.co.ke with your account details and reason. We process refunds within 14 business days via M-Pesa or bank transfer.

Questions? Contact us at legal@nyumbamanager.co.ke
