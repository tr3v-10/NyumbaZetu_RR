Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

This policy explains how NyumbaManager uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They enable the platform to remember your preferences and session state.

Cookies We Use

  • session (essential): Maintains your login session. Deleted when you close your browser or log out. Cannot be disabled without breaking authentication.
  • csrf_token (essential): Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks. Required for all forms.
  • nyumba_theme (functional): Remembers your light/dark theme preference. Stored in localStorage, not a cookie. Persists indefinitely.
  • sidebar_collapsed (functional): Remembers whether you've collapsed the sidebar. Stored in localStorage.

No Advertising or Analytics Cookies

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising cookies. We do not share cookie data with advertisers.

Managing Cookies

You can clear cookies via your browser settings. Clearing essential cookies will log you out. You cannot opt out of essential cookies while using the platform.
