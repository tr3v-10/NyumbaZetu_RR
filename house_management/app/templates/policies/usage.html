Software Usage Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective for all NyumbaManager users

This policy governs acceptable use of NyumbaManager software and APIs.

Permitted Use

You may use NyumbaManager solely for legitimate property management purposes: managing your own properties, paying rent for your tenancy, or performing caretaker/agent duties for properties you are assigned to.

Prohibited Activities

  • Attempting to access other users' data without authorisation
  • Automated scraping, crawling, or bulk data extraction
  • Reverse engineering or decompiling the software
  • Using the platform to facilitate money laundering or fraud
  • Creating multiple accounts to circumvent rate limits or restrictions
  • Uploading malware, ransomware, or harmful files
  • Impersonating landlords, tenants, or NyumbaManager staff
  • Using the API without authorised credentials

Rate Limits

API endpoints are rate-limited to 200 requests/hour for standard users. M-Pesa STK Push endpoints are limited to 10 requests/minute. Exceeding these limits will result in temporary blocks.

Consequences of Violation

Violations may result in immediate account suspension, permanent ban, reporting to relevant authorities, and civil or criminal liability under Kenyan law including the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018.

Questions? Contact us at legal@nyumbamanager.co.ke
